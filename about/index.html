Acotar Court Quiz is an independent fan project inspired by Sarah J. Maas’s A Court of Thorns and Roses. It offers three original personality quizzes, written in a playful, share-with-your-friends style.

How we score your answers

Each quiz has 12 questions with four answers each. Every answer gives three affinity points to one outcome and one point to another. For each outcome, we divide earned points by the largest number that outcome could earn across all 12 questions. This prevents a result with more scoring opportunities from winning simply because it appears more often.

The highest normalized score wins. Equal scores use a stable result order, and the result screen identifies the tie with the next outcome. The runner-up is the next-highest score. We do not display compatibility percentages because the scoring has not been scientifically validated.

Fated mate preferences

The optional all-characters, men, or women selection limits which outcomes can win. It does not change question wording or collect information about your identity. These are imagined romantic connections, independent of canon pairings.

Editorial approach

All question text, result descriptions, and decorative illustrations were created for this site. Court associations and character profiles are subjective fan interpretations, not official descriptions. Light character and setting references appear, but we avoid major plot reveals.

For the actual books and official reading information, visit Sarah J. Maas’s official ACOTAR page.

Independent and unofficial

This site is not affiliated with Sarah J. Maas, Bloomsbury, or BuzzFeed. It does not reproduce book passages, covers, or official character artwork. It is an entertainment project, not a psychological or relationship assessment.
