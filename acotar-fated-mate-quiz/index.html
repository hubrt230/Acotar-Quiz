How this quiz works

Choose one response for each scenario. You can go back and change an answer before revealing your result. Each choice adds weighted affinity points; totals are normalized against the maximum available for each outcome. No answer is objectively better than another.

The fated mate quiz includes men and women, with an optional filter before you start. Results imagine a connection with you; they are not claims about canon pairings or real-world relationship compatibility.
