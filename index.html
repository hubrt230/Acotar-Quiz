A few things to know

What is the ACOTAR court quiz?

It is a free, fan-made personality quiz inspired by A Court of Thorns and Roses. Twelve everyday and fantasy scenarios match your preferences with Night, Day, Dawn, Spring, Summer, Autumn, or Winter Court. No book trivia required.

Do these quizzes contain spoilers?

There are light character and world references from across the series, including character names and general personality traits. We avoid major plot reveals, but readers who want to know absolutely nothing beyond book one may prefer to return later.

How are my results chosen?

Each answer contributes to two possible outcomes. We normalize the totals so outcomes with more scoring opportunities do not automatically win. Your strongest affinity becomes your result. These are original fan interpretations for entertainment, not official lore judgments or scientific tests.

Can I choose who appears in the fated mate quiz?

Yes. Choose all characters, men, or women before starting. Your preference changes the result pool. The quiz explores fictional chemistry and does not follow canon relationship pairings.

Is it free, and do I need an account?

All three quizzes are free. There is no signup. Your progress stays in session storage in your browser tab, and no quiz answers are sent to a server by this site.
