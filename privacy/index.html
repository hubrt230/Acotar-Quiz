Your answers stay with you.

These quizzes calculate results inside your browser. This site does not send quiz answers to a server or ask for a name, email address, or account.

Browser storage

We use sessionStorage to remember your progress in the current browser tab. Starting fresh or retaking replaces that quiz’s stored answers. Closing the tab normally clears session storage, although browser session-restoration settings may preserve it. If storage is blocked, the quiz still works, but progress may not survive a reload.

Sharing

The Share button uses your device’s sharing interface when available, or copies a result link. Only the result name and public result page are shared, not your individual answers. Nothing is posted automatically.

Hosting and external links

The site contains no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, or third-party font requests. The hosting provider may process standard web requests, including IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. External links and any app you choose to share through have their own policies.
